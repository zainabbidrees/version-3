## Section — Good to know (FAQ)
Eyebrow: Good to know
**Common Ordering Questions**
The practical details of how a store works day to day. Answers are confirmed with your team during setup.
1. Is there a minimum order quantity? — It depends on the product and decoration — we set any minimums with you during setup.
2. Can individual employees place their own orders? — Yes — your team orders what they need directly through the store.
3. Who is responsible for payment? — However your program is set up — the company can pay centrally, or employees can pay at checkout.
4. Where can orders be shipped? — Wherever you need — a single location, multiple locations, or individual employee addresses.
5. Are products kept in stock or made after each order? — Both — some items are stocked for quick turnaround, others are produced after ordering.
